ABOUT THE FOUNDER

From aviation problem-solving to automotive product development.

Tyler Hall founded Hall Integrated Systems to turn recurring car audio and 12V installation problems into original physical products developed under the company’s own brand.

Concept visualization for product-development planning. CAD model and prototype print pending.

Founder Story

A difficult chapter created room for a new direction.

I created Hall Integrated Systems during one of the most challenging periods of my life. My aviation employer, Silver Airways, ceased operations. Soon afterward, an important personal relationship ended unexpectedly. Life as I knew it came to a halt, and every day became a struggle to get out of bed.

I interviewed with other companies, but my heart was not in simply replacing one job with another. I needed something constructive to work toward, so I returned to something I had wanted to do for a long time: upgrade the sound system in my Audi A3 sedan.

The original Audi system was fine—but “fine” is not something I typically tolerate for very long when a cleaner, better solution should be possible.

The project that started it

I began by ordering four matching coaxial speakers for the Audi’s doors. When I was 16, my dad and I had replaced the speakers in my first car, a 1992 Toyota Paseo—the same year I was born. I remember thinking, “I’ve done this before. I know this is an Audi, but how different could it be?”

I soon learned that my Audi simply did not want to be modified. Coming from an aviation background, that was not an acceptable final answer.

The obstacles became product ideas

If a mistake could be made, I made it. If something could go wrong, I found it. Each setback exposed another fitment, mounting, wiring, access, or serviceability problem that a purpose-built component could solve.

By then, I had accumulated many of the same hand and diagnostic tools used in Volkswagen and Audi specialty work. What I did not have were the small, practical parts needed to make the installation cleaner, more maintainable, and easier to repeat.

The 3D-printer moment

One day I caught myself thinking, “This would be so much easier if I just had a 3D printer.” That lightbulb moment changed the direction of the project. Product idea after product idea followed: cable combs, wire-routing clips, speaker spacer rings, fuse-holder bases, DSP platforms, amplifier standoffs, trim pieces, and low-profile brackets.

The goal is not to create decorative parts or operate as a print service. The goal is to develop original Hall Integrated Systems products around real automotive installation problems.

Building Hall Integrated Systems

I slowly began assembling the pieces of Hall Integrated Systems, a bootstrapped Florida LLC focused on company-owned physical products. I currently operate the company on my own, with the goal of building a team as the product pipeline matures.

I acquired the company’s first 3D printer, a QIDI Plus4, on July 21, 2026. I am deliberately holding CAD and prototype production until proper commercial software licensing is in place. Once secured, HIS-CA-001A will move from its pre-CAD specification into modeling, prototype printing, dimensional and fit review, revision, and small-batch manufacturing preparation.
