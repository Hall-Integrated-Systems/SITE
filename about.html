ABOUT HALL INTEGRATED SYSTEMS

A company built around practical automotive problems.

Tyler Hall founded Hall Integrated Systems to turn recurring car-audio and 12V installation constraints into original physical products developed under the company’s own brand.

Commercial CAD access established; HIS-CA-001A CAD modeling is in progress; prototype print and validation remain pending.

FOUNDER STORY

A difficult chapter created room for a new direction.

I created Hall Integrated Systems during one of the most challenging periods of my life. My aviation employer, Silver Airways, ceased operations. Soon afterward, an important personal relationship ended unexpectedly. Life as I knew it came to a halt, and every day became a struggle to get out of bed.

I interviewed with other companies, but my heart was not in simply replacing one job with another. I needed something constructive to work toward, so I returned to something I had wanted to do for a long time: upgrade the sound system in my Audi A3 sedan.

The original system was fine—but “fine” is not something I tolerate for long when a cleaner, better solution should be possible.

01

The project that started it

I began by ordering four matching coaxial speakers for the Audi’s doors. When I was 16, my dad and I had replaced the speakers in my first car, a 1992 Toyota Paseo—the same year I was born. I remember thinking, “I’ve done this before. I know this is an Audi, but how different could it be?”

I soon learned that the car did not want to be modified. Coming from an aviation background, that was not an acceptable final answer.

02

The obstacles became product ideas

Each setback exposed another fitment, mounting, wiring, access, or serviceability problem that a purpose-built component could solve. The missing pieces were often small, practical parts that could make the installation cleaner, more maintainable, and easier to repeat.

Cable combs, wire-routing clips, speaker spacer rings, fuse-holder bases, DSP platforms, amplifier standoffs, trim pieces, and low-profile brackets followed as product directions.

03

Building Hall Integrated Systems

I began assembling Hall Integrated Systems as a bootstrapped Florida LLC focused on company-owned physical products. I currently operate the company on my own, with the goal of building a team as the product pipeline matures.

In-house prototyping capability now supports the physical side of the company-owned development workflow. Commercial CAD access established the digital design path. HIS-CA-001A’s REV-A working decision is documented and CAD modeling is in progress; the representative coupon, complete prototype print, dimensional review, fit evaluation, revision outcome, and small-batch preparation remain pending.
