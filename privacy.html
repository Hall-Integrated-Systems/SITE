Privacy

Clear handling of website inquiry information.

Hall Integrated Systems keeps privacy information simple and focused on how inquiry details are used.

Information collected

Contact form submissions may collect your name, email address, subject, and message.

Contact form submissions are processed through Hall Integrated Systems' Azure-hosted backend and Microsoft 365 mail services to deliver messages.

This information is used only to respond to inquiries and evaluate automotive project requests related to vehicle electronics hardware, prototype components, and installation-focused product development.

Website analytics

This website uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through behavioral metrics such as clicks, scrolling, and page interaction data. This information helps improve the website and user experience. Microsoft may process this data in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

How information is handled

Hall Integrated Systems does not sell personal information.

No payment processing is handled on this website.
