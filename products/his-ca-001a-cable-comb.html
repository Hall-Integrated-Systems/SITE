HIS-CA-001A

HIS-CA-001A 4-Wire Speaker Cable Comb.

A company-designed, low-profile cable-management product concept for clean 14-16 AWG speaker wire routing on amplifier racks and automotive installation panels.

Prototype Concept

Overview

HIS-CA-001A is the first company-owned product concept in the Hall Integrated Systems development queue. The concept uses four open rounded wire channels, a center countersunk screw-hole idea, two long zip-tie slot concepts, rounded corners, chamfered edges, and subtle PETG/FDM-style product design language.

Problem Solved

Custom car-audio amplifier racks and installation panels often need clean parallel routing for speaker wiring without relying on improvised zip-tie placement alone. This concept is intended to improve wire organization, maintenance access, and finished appearance while keeping the part low-profile.

Concept Features

  • Four open wire channels for 14-16 AWG speaker wiring
  • Center screw-mount concept with countersunk direction
  • Dual zip-tie slot concept for secondary retention
  • Target material: PETG
  • Low-profile geometry for amplifier rack and panel layouts
  • Rounded corners and chamfered edges for a finished installation-hardware look

Development Status

Current status: AI concept visualization and a pre-CAD specification reference are complete. Hall Integrated Systems acquired its first 3D printer, a QIDI Plus4, on July 21, 2026. CAD modeling, dimensional validation, and the first prototype print remain pending proper commercial Autodesk Fusion access and print workflow commissioning.

Planned Manufacturing Direction

The planned direction is 3D-printed prototype development using PETG as the target material. This is a prototype concept only and not available as a production product.
