Company-owned development process

Eight gates between an idea and a small batch.

Hall Integrated Systems uses this process for its own automotive hardware pipeline. Each gate records what changed and what remains unverified.

  1. Define the installation problem

    Record the automotive packaging, routing, access, or mounting constraint.

  2. Document a working design decision

    Choose the current direction and state what has been deferred.

  3. Build and inspect the CAD model

    Compare visible geometry with the documented decision before calling the model complete.

  4. Measure critical interfaces and print a representative coupon

    Check cable, fastener, mounting-depth, clearance, and access inputs before the complete part.

  5. Print the complete prototype

    Document the actual material, print orientation, and resulting physical part.

  6. Evaluate fit, retention, vibration, pull, access, and serviceability

    Record only the checks actually performed and the conditions used.

  7. Record revision decisions

    Connect observed results to retained, changed, or deferred features.

  8. Prepare for a small batch

    Begin preparation only after the relevant design and validation evidence exists.
