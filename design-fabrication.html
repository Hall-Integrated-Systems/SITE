DESIGN & FABRICATION

How Hall Integrated Systems develops its products.

This internal design and fabrication workflow turns real automotive installation constraints into company-owned product concepts, CAD models, prototypes, and manufacturing-preparation revisions.

CAD-to-print direction

Design energy stays tied to physical parts.

Visual concept work, CAD modeling, printed prototypes, and fit revisions support Hall Integrated Systems’ own branded product pipeline rather than outside client design or contract manufacturing.

From install problem to physical component

Real-world automotive audio and 12V electronics layouts expose recurring hardware problems: messy wire routing, awkward component mounting, speaker fitment constraints, fuse-holder placement, and maintenance access issues. Hall Integrated Systems turns those problems into original product concepts intended for future manufacturing and commercialization under its own brand.

Fabrication focus

Current development focuses on practical 3D-printed installation hardware including cable combs, wire-routing clips, speaker spacer rings, fuse-holder bases, DSP mounting platforms, amplifier standoffs, trim pieces, and low-profile brackets.

Workflow

Development workflow

  1. Identify install problem
  2. Define product concept
  3. Generate visual prototype
  4. Create CAD model
  5. Print prototype
  6. Test fit and revise
  7. Prepare small-batch production
