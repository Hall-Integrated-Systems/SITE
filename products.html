Products & Prototypes

3D-printed automotive audio installation hardware in development.

Hall Integrated Systems develops prototype components for cleaner, more reliable car audio and 12V electronics builds. Product concepts are early-stage and are not presented as finished inventory.

Product Development Focus

The current product direction centers on 3D-printed automotive installation hardware: cable combs, wire-routing clips, speaker spacer rings, fuse-holder bases, DSP mounting platforms, amplifier standoffs, brackets, and installation aids.

Each concept is intended to solve a practical installation problem: cleaner wiring, improved maintenance access, better component mounting, or a more finished OEM-plus layout.

Development Truthfulness

Products shown here are prototype concepts, planned parts, or in-development components. CAD modeling, prototype printing, fit testing, and refinement are part of the planned workflow before any production or sales claim is made.

Featured Product Concept

Automotive Installation Hardware in Development

HIS-CA-001A

Prototype Concept

4-Wire Speaker Cable Comb

Category: Car Audio Installation Hardware

HIS-CA-001A is a prototype 4-wire speaker cable comb designed to organize 14-16 AWG speaker wiring on amplifier racks and automotive installation panels. The concept is intended to improve wire routing, maintenance access, and finished appearance in custom car-audio builds.

Target material PETG
Mounting concept Center screw hole plus two zip-tie slots
Development status AI concept complete; CAD model pending; prototype print pending
Compatibility note Designed around 14-16 AWG speaker wiring for amplifier rack and installation-panel wire management.

Key Concept Features

  • Four open wire channels for 14-16 AWG speaker wiring
  • Center screw-mount concept
  • Dual zip-tie slot concept
  • Low-profile PETG design target
  • Designed for amplifier rack and installation-panel wire management
  • CAD model pending
  • Prototype print pending
